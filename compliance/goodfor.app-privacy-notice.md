## A. Privacy-policy paragraph (paste into goodfor.app's privacy policy)

> **Website experience analytics.** With your consent, GoodFor uses a first-party experience
> monitor to detect technical and usability problems on our own pages — for example broken links
> and buttons, JavaScript errors, form-validation friction, broken images and slow-loading
> resources — so we can fix them. This is collected **only after you accept the "Statistics"
> category** in our cookie banner. We collect **aggregate defect information only**: the type of
> problem, the page path (without any query-string details), a scrubbed element selector, a
> truncated and redacted error message, and form-field names/types and whether a field was valid —
> **never the values you type**. We do **not** record your screen, your keystrokes or mouse
> movements, we do **not** store your IP address (we use only a salted, truncated, non-identifying
> hash), and we do **not** create any persistent or cross-site identifier or use this data for
> advertising or profiling. We rely on your **consent** (UK GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)) and the Privacy and
> Electronic Communications Regulations (PECR). Raw event data is automatically deleted within
> **72 hours**; only anonymous, aggregated trends are kept. You can **withdraw consent at any time**
> via our cookie settings, and we honour Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track signals;
> withdrawing consent stops collection and deletes the raw data we hold for this site.
> Questions: **[CONTACT EMAIL]**.

## B. Statistics-category line (paste into Complianz → Consent → Statistics description)

> **Website experience monitoring.** Helps us detect broken links, errors and form problems on
> our pages so we can fix them. First-party only, aggregate data, **no recording of what you type**,
> no IP stored, no advertising or cross-site tracking. Raw data deleted within 72 hours. Off until
> you accept; you can withdraw any time.
