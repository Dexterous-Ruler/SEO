# Experience Monitor — privacy notice snippet (copy-paste per site)

> UK-focused. Add the paragraph below to the site's **privacy policy**, and the banner
> line to the **cookie/consent banner**. Replace `[SITE NAME]` and `[CONTACT EMAIL]`.
> The beacon is **cookie-consent gated** — it only runs after the visitor accepts the
> analytics category — and stays inert until the site is armed.

---

## A. Privacy-policy paragraph (paste into your privacy policy)

> **Website experience analytics.** With your consent, [SITE NAME] uses a first-party
> experience monitor to detect technical and usability problems on our own pages — for
> example broken links and buttons, JavaScript errors, form-validation friction, broken
> images and slow-loading resources — so we can fix them. This is collected **only after
> you accept the "analytics" category** in our cookie banner. We collect **aggregate
> defect information only**: the type of problem, the page path (without any query-string
> details), a scrubbed element selector, a truncated and redacted error message, and
> form-field names/types and whether a field was valid — **never the values you type**.
> We do **not** record your screen, your keystrokes or mouse movements, we do **not**
> store your IP address (we use only a salted, truncated, non-identifying hash), and we
> do **not** create any persistent or cross-site identifier or use this data for
> advertising or profiling. We rely on your **consent** (UK GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)) and the
> Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations (PECR). Raw event data is automatically
> deleted within **72 hours**; only anonymous, aggregated trends are kept. You can
> **withdraw consent at any time** via our cookie settings, and we honour Global Privacy
> Control and Do Not Track signals; withdrawing consent stops collection and deletes the
> raw data we hold for this site. Questions: [CONTACT EMAIL].

## B. Cookie/consent banner line (paste into your banner's analytics category)

> **Analytics — website experience monitoring.** Helps us detect broken links, errors
> and form problems on our pages so we can fix them. First-party only, aggregate data,
> **no recording of what you type**, no IP stored, no advertising or cross-site tracking.
> Raw data deleted within 72 hours. Off until you accept; you can withdraw any time.
